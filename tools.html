Free Calculators for Housing Societies

Quick answers to everyday questions in society accounts: interest on late maintenance, minimum sinking and repair fund, and whether a payment needs TDS.

Interest on Late Maintenance

Simple interest, day-wise, from the due date to the payment date.

Rule 106C-12 of the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules (amended 2026) caps interest at 12% per year simple interest. Use the rate fixed by your general body. Some societies charge interest by full month instead of by day; the method in your resolution applies. Read what changed in 2026.

Sinking & Repair Fund

Minimum yearly contribution under Rule 106C-11.

The construction cost should be the figure certified by the society's architect. If your society does not have one, we can help you arrange it.

TDS Checker for Society Payments

Does this payment need TDS? Income-tax Act, 2025, tax year 2026-27.

Thresholds and rates as per section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 for tax year 2026-27. This is a quick check, not tax advice. Lower-deduction certificates and special cases are not covered. We handle TDS for societies.

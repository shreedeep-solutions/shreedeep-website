👥 Members

Flats and members: names, contacts, area, parking, nominees. Add, edit, import from Excel.

फ्लॅट आणि सभासद: नावे, संपर्क, क्षेत्रफळ, पार्किंग, नॉमिनी. नवीन जोडा, बदला, Excel मधून आयात करा.

Step by step
  1. + Add Member: flat, wing, name, mobile, email, area, parking, nominee. Save.
  2. Bring in a list: 📥 Import, 📋 Paste, 🤖 From any list (photo / PDF / Excel), 📷 Register photo, or 📥 From ADDA / MyGate.
  3. 🔤 Tidy names fixes ALL CAPS and adds Mr./Mrs. (✨ Ask AI for names it is unsure of). 📇 Missing contacts lists members without mobile or email.
  4. 👯 Find duplicates / 🔀 Merge duplicates clean double entries. 🔁 Flat transfer records a sale.

📕 Registers (I, J, Share, Nomination)

The I, J, Share and Nomination registers — the same Society Register Manager you know, inside S4S. Flats and owners come from Members and Flat Transfers. I Forms (legal page or A4), registers in Excel (English / मराठी / both), share certificates, letters and resolutions are in the Print / Export sections of the register screens.

आय, जे, शेअर आणि नॉमिनेशन रजिस्टर — तेच सोसायटी रजिस्टर मॅनेजर, आता S4S मध्ये. फ्लॅट आणि सभासद Members व Flat Transfers मधून येतात. आय फॉर्म (लीगल किंवा A4), Excel रजिस्टर (इंग्रजी / मराठी / दोन्ही), शेअर सर्टिफिकेट, पत्रे आणि ठराव येथेच मिळतात.

Step by step
  1. I, J, Share and Nomination registers, share certificates and I Forms (legal page or A4). Members and transfers come from S4S. ↻ Reload picks up new members.
Buttons on this screen
↻ ReloadLoad the registers again (also picks up new members and transfers from S4S)

🪪 Member KYC

Step by step
  1. Track each member's documents: possession letter, PAN, Aadhaar (masked), photograph, undertaking, police verification (if let out) and bank NOC / loan papers.
  2. Open a member to tick what is received; the percentage shows how complete each file is, so you can see who is pending.

📱 Member App

Requests from members on the app (payments, updates, NOC, bookings) and members joining the app.

ॲपवरून सभासदांच्या विनंत्या (पेमेंट, बदल, NOC, बुकिंग) आणि ॲपवर नवीन सभासद.

Step by step
  1. Requests from members (payments, change of details, NOC, bookings) wait here — approve or reject each.
  2. 📨 Invite members sends the app link. Tabs show members on the app, polls and facilities.
Buttons on this screen
📨 Invite membersSend the member app link.
↻ Update nowSend the latest bills, receipts and notices to the member app now.
📊 Polls & RSVPsPolls and RSVPs for members.
📁 DocumentsSociety documents shared with members on the app.

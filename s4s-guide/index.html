Getting started

What is Solutions4Society?

Solutions4Society (S4S) is accounting and management software made for co-operative housing societies in Maharashtra. It keeps the books (receipts, payments, journals), makes maintenance bills with interest, imports bank statements, prints every register and final account, and gives members, committee and security staff their own apps.

It runs in the browser on any PC, laptop or phone (Chrome, Edge, Safari). Open sas.shreedeepsolutions.com. Nothing needs to be installed, but you can install it like an app: in Chrome press the install icon in the address bar (PC) or ⋮ → Add to Home screen (phone).

Your data is saved online the moment you press Save. The top bar shows ✓ Saved and ● Live. If the internet drops, keep working — entries are kept on the device and sent as soon as the connection comes back.

Signing in and choosing a role

Sign in with Google or with your email and password. The firm (or the society admin) must first add your email in Team & Access.

If your email has more than one role, “Who are you today?” lists them: Accounts, Committee, Member, Security, Vendor. Open one here, or ↗ in a new tab — every tab keeps its own role. ⇄ Switch in the top bar brings the list back. A coloured line at the top of the screen shows which role you are in.

Accountants of the firm first see All Societies. Click a society to open it. The society name, the financial year (e.g. FY 2025-26) and the role are always shown at the top. Click the year to change it.

Finding your way around

Menu (left side on PC, ☰ on phones): Home, Entries, Billing, Banking, Members & Registers, Accounts & Reports, Office & Settings. Some screens have tabs at the top (for example Receipt · Payment).

🔍 Search (Ctrl+K): type a flat number, member name, voucher number, amount or screen name and press Enter.

⬆ Upload: drop any file — bank statement, member list, bill photos, MAXX or Tally data. S4S works out what it is and opens the right screen. You can also drag a file anywhere onto the window.

Buttons at the top right of each screen: the main buttons for that screen. Less-used ones are inside ⋯ More.

🔔 Bell: things that need attention (approvals, cheques to deposit, reminders). 🌙 switches dark mode.

Right-click a row (PC) to edit the entry or bill, or open the ledger. Shift + right-click gives the normal browser menu.

Using S4S on a phone

The bottom bar has the most-used screens. The phone Back button moves to the previous screen (and closes an open window first). Press Back twice on the home screen to leave.

Pull down at the top of a screen to refresh it. Long-press an amount to copy it. Tap a mobile number in a table to call.

▤ Cards shows a long table as cards that fit the phone. 📤 Share picture sends a report as an image on WhatsApp.

Amount boxes open the number keypad and accept sums like 2500*3 or 1200+450 — the result is filled in when you leave the box. While you type, the Save button stays visible above the keyboard.

✨ AI in S4S

Every screen has a ✨ AI button: Explain this screen, Check for mistakes, Committee summary (English + मराठी), Draft a letter and Ask anything.

AI also reads cheque photos, bills, bank statements, passbook photos, FD receipts, old registers and member lists, and sorts bank statement lines into heads.

Privacy: member names, mobile numbers and emails are hidden from the AI by default (Settings → 🔒 AI privacy). AI answers can be wrong — always look before you save.

Getting help inside the app

❓ on any screen explains it in English and मराठी. Settings → 📘 User manual opens this guide.

The guided tour (Help → Tour) walks you through the main screens for your role.

Made a mistake? ↶ History (Ctrl+Shift+Z) undoes any of your last 20 saved entries. Every change is also kept in the Audit Trail.

Your monthly routine

  1. Start of the month
    • Billing → Bills → ⚡ Generate Bills for the period (monthly, bi-monthly or quarterly). Check the totals, then print, email or share them.
    • Members who receive a printed bill can scan the My account QR to see their own ledger.
  2. Every day / as money comes in
    • Entries → Receipt (F6) for each payment, or Bulk Receipts for many flats at once.
    • Cheques: Banking → Cheque Deposit → pay-in slip → ✓ Mark deposited.
    • Payments (F5) for every expense, with TDS where it applies.
  3. When the bank statement arrives
    • Banking → Statement upload → choose the bank → upload the bank's Excel / PDF or read it with AI.
    • Check the 🗂 Sort heads step (new heads are created for you), then post. Entries already in the books are skipped.
    • Banking → Bank Reconciliation to tick what is still pending and print the BRS.
  4. Follow-up
    • Billing → Dues / Outstanding → reminders by letter, email or WhatsApp; waive interest only with a reason.
  5. Month end
    • Dashboard → Month close card. Year-End → Health check shows mistakes and how to fix them. Print the reports the committee wants.
  6. Year end
    • Year-End → Checklist → Close & locks → Notes to accounts → Complete Books (one PDF for the auditor) → Form O after the audit.

Common questions

I saved an entry by mistake. How do I undo it?

↶ History (Ctrl+Shift+Z) lists your last 20 entries — undo any of them. Older entries: open the entry from the Day Book and delete or edit it.

The bank statement has a payee that is not in my heads.

In the 🗂 Sort heads step choose ➕ New head — it is created for you when you continue. Keep 📌 ticked and it is remembered next time.

A member says the balance is wrong.

Open Member Ledger → ❓ Why this balance. It shows how the balance is made up: opening balance, bills, supplementary bills, interest, receipts and adjustments. Send the 🔗 Share link so the member sees the same ledger.

Bills show the wrong charge for one flat.

Billing Setup → change the amount for that flat → open the bill and edit it (or re-run the bills for that period).

Can I work without internet?

Yes, for a short time. Entries are kept on the device and sent when the connection comes back. Do not clear the browser data while offline.

The screen looks old after an update.

Press Ctrl+F5 on a PC, or close and open the app again on a phone.

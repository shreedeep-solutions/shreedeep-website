🧾 Bills

Make the maintenance bills for a period, print them, send them, and see each bill.

एका कालावधीची मेंटेनन्स बिले तयार करा, प्रिंट करा, पाठवा आणि प्रत्येक बिल पहा.

Step by step
  1. Press ⚡ Generate Bills. Choose the period (from / to), bill date and due date, then Preview.
  2. Check the totals and a few sample bills. Press Generate — one bill per flat is made with arrears and interest.
  3. Open a bill to view, edit (amount per head), print or send it. Print or email all bills from the bill run card.
  4. Use ⊕ Supplementary Bill for an extra charge for many flats, 🧾 One-off bill for one flat, and 📌 Important notes for a message printed on the bills.
  5. 🔗 Account QR for all members makes the “My account” links in one go (they are also made automatically when bills are printed with the setting on).

⊕ Supplementary BillExtra bill for many flats (e.g. painting, festival) outside the regular bill.
📌 Important notesA note printed on the bills for one or more months.
🧾 One-off billA bill for one flat only.
🧮 More billing toolsOther billing tools (bill numbers, re-run, undo a bill run…).

🧮 Billing Setup › Charges per flat

The charges each flat pays every period (per head). Bills are made from this.

प्रत्येक फ्लॅटचे प्रत्येक कालावधीचे चार्जेस (हेडनुसार). बिले यावरून तयार होतात.

Step by step
  1. Every flat is a row and every billing head is a column. Type the amount each flat pays per period.
  2. ↧ Fill a column puts the same amount for all flats; 📐 By area rate works out area × rate; 🚗 Parking & Non-Occupancy adds those charges.
  3. 🧾 Billing heads adds or reorders heads. 📝 Standing particulars are lines printed on every bill.
  4. Save. The next bills use these amounts. 📚 Versions keeps old rates so earlier bills are not changed.

🚗 Parking & Non-OccupancyParking and non-occupancy charges.

📚 VersionsOlder rate versions of the charges.

🧮 Billing Setup › Rules & extras

Step by step
  1. Set the rules used by the bills: late fee, grace days, minimum amount ignored, prompt-payment rebate, annual advance discount, pro-rata dates, bill numbering, reminders and Hindi bills.
  2. Each card saves on its own. Interest itself (method and rate) is set in Settings → Billing.

💰 Collections › Follow-up

Collection summary — billed, received and pending.

वसुली सारांश — बिल केलेले, मिळालेले आणि बाकी.

Step by step
  1. See billed, received and pending for each period. Tabs: Risk & forecast, Reminders, Instalments, Recovery, Rules & tools.
  2. Use Instalments to record a payment plan agreed with a member; Recovery to track Section 101 / legal steps.

💰 Collections › Daily collection

Step by step

  2. 🧾 Cheque deposit slip makes the slip for the cheques in the list.

🔴 Dues / Outstanding

Who owes how much and for how long. Print reminders or send WhatsApp messages from here.

कोणाकडे किती आणि किती दिवसांपासून थकबाकी आहे. येथून स्मरणपत्रे प्रिंट करा किंवा WhatsApp पाठवा.

Step by step
  1. Choose As on date and a minimum amount; the list shows who owes how much and for how long.
  2. Tick members and send ✉ Reminder letters, 📧 Email reminders or 💬 WhatsApp. ⏰ Schedule sends reminders automatically.
  3. ✂ Waive interest (with reason / resolution) when the committee approves.
  4. 💰 Risk, reminders & recovery opens the follow-up screen.

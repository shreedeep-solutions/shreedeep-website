Step by step
  1. Type what you are looking for (e.g. “interest”, “defaulters”, “FD”) — every matching report is listed. Click to open.

👤 Member Ledger

One member's account: bills, receipts, interest and the running balance.

एका सभासदाचे खाते: बिले, पावत्या, व्याज आणि चालू शिल्लक.

Step by step
  1. Pick the member (flat no. or name).
  2. See every bill, receipt, interest and the running balance. Tick “Show charges of each bill” for head-wise detail. Click a line to edit it.
  3. 🔗 Share link sends the member their live ledger. 📄 No dues certificate, 📝 Form 14, 💰 Amount to clear dues and ✂ Waive interest are here too.
Buttons on this screen
🖨 All membersPrint every member's ledger.
📥 All members (Excel)All members' ledgers in one Excel file.
🖨 PrintPrint / save as PDF.
🔗 Share linkSend the member a link to their live ledger.
💰 Amount to clear duesExact amount to clear all dues today, with interest.
📄 No dues certificatePrint a no-dues certificate for the member.
📄 Statement (any dates)Member statement for any dates.
📝 Form 14Form 14 (member's dues statement).
✂ Waive interestWaive interest with the reason / committee resolution.
🕘 Change historyEvery change made to this member's entries.

📖 General Ledger

Any account head's ledger with running balance.

कोणत्याही हेडचे लेजर, चालू शिल्लकेसह.

Step by step
  1. Choose the head (and dates) — every entry with running balance. Click a line to open the entry. 🖨 Print all ledgers prints every head.
Buttons on this screen
🖨 Print all ledgersPrint every head's ledger.

⚖️ Trial Balance

Trial Balance — totals of every head; debits must equal credits.

ट्रायल बॅलन्स — प्रत्येक हेडची एकूण रक्कम; डेबिट आणि क्रेडिट समान असले पाहिजेत.

Step by step
  1. Choose the date. Debit and credit totals must match. 🔎 Check finds problems. Print in English, मराठी or हिंदी; 📥 Excel with formulas; 📌 Year-end provisions for accruals.
Buttons on this screen
🔎 CheckCheck the Trial Balance for problems.
🖨 PrintPrint / save as PDF.
🖨 मराठी (formal print)Formal print in Marathi.
🖨 हिंदी (formal print)Formal print in Hindi.
📥 Excel with formulasExcel with live formulas (totals add up in Excel).
✍ Signature pageAdd the signatures page (Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer, auditor).
📌 Year-end provisionsYear-end outstanding and prepaid expenses (e.g. audit fees, electricity bill not yet paid) as journal entries.

📊 Income & Expenditure

Income & Expenditure account — income minus expenses for the year.

उत्पन्न व खर्च पत्रक — वर्षाचे उत्पन्न वजा खर्च.

Step by step
  1. Choose the period. Income minus expenditure gives the surplus or deficit. Tick “Previous year column” to compare. Print, or 🖨 Form N for the statutory format.
Buttons on this screen
🖨 PrintPrint / save as PDF.

🖨 हिंदी (formal print)Formal print in Hindi.
🖨 Form NFinal accounts in the statutory Form N.
✍ Signature pageAdd the signatures page (Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer, auditor).

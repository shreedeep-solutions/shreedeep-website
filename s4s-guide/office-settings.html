📅 Year-End › Close & locks

Close the year, lock months, carry balances forward.

वर्ष बंद करा, महिने लॉक करा, शिल्लक पुढे न्या.

Step by step
  1. Lock months that are final (no one can change them). Close the year: balances are carried forward to the next year. The bottom card shows whether later years are in step.

🤝 Meetings

Meeting agenda and minutes.

सभेचा अजेंडा आणि इतिवृत्त.

Step by step
  1. + New meeting or 📋 AGM (standard agenda). Write the agenda (Subject No. 1, 2 …), send the notice.
  2. After the meeting: 🤖 Minutes from notes / recording, then the 📚 Minute book and 📜 Resolutions register. 📘 Bye-law library for references.

📚 Minute bookAll minutes in one book.
📜 Resolutions registerAll resolutions passed.

Society settings: billing, interest, printing, templates, email and display.

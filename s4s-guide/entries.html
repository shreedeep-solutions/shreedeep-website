📥 Receipt · Payment › 📥 Receipt

Record money received — pick the member (or type the flat number), the amount fills in by itself. Enter moves to the next box and saves at the end. Picking a cash or bank account here (e.g. cash withdrawn from bank) saves it as a contra entry automatically.

मिळालेली रक्कम नोंदवा — सभासद निवडा (किंवा फ्लॅट नंबर टाइप करा), रक्कम आपोआप भरली जाते. Enter दाबल्यावर पुढच्या बॉक्सवर जाते आणि शेवटी सेव्ह होते.

Step by step
  1. Press F6 (or open Entries → Receipt).
  2. Check the Date and Received in (cash or which bank).
  3. In Received from type the flat number or name and pick the member — the amount due fills in by itself.
  4. Change the Amount if needed. Choose the Mode; for a cheque fill Cheque No., Cheque date and Drawn on bank.
  5. Press Enter through the boxes — the last Enter saves. The receipt number is given automatically and the receipt can be printed or sent.
  6. Cash taken out of the bank? Pick the bank in “Received from” — it is saved as a contra entry automatically.

📥 Receipt · Payment › 📤 Payment

Record money paid out — pick the expense head, amount, mode and cheque number. F8 gives ready narrations. To deposit cash or move money between banks, pick the bank/cash account as the account — it is saved as a contra entry.

दिलेली रक्कम नोंदवा — खर्चाचा हेड, रक्कम, पद्धत आणि चेक नंबर निवडा. F8 ने तयार नॅरेशन मिळते.

Step by step
  1. Press F5 (or Entries → Payment).
  2. Choose Paid from (cash / bank), then Paid to — the expense head or vendor.
  3. Enter the Amount, Mode and cheque / UTR number. Press F8 for a ready narration.
  4. If TDS applies, tick Deduct TDS, choose the section — the TDS amount, party name and PAN fill in; the bank amount is reduced automatically.
  5. Save (Enter at the end or Ctrl+S). To put cash into the bank, pick the bank as “Paid to” — it becomes a contra entry.

🔁 Journal

Adjustments that are not cash or bank — e.g. waiving interest, correcting a head, year-end entries.

कॅश किंवा बँक नसलेल्या दुरुस्त्या — उदा. व्याज माफ करणे, हेड बदलणे, वर्षअखेरीच्या नोंदी.

Step by step
  1. Use for adjustments without cash or bank: waiving interest, correcting a head, provisions, year-end entries.
  2. Enter the date and narration, then add debit and credit lines (head or member) until the totals match.
  3. Save. The journal number is given automatically.

📒 Day Book

Every entry in date order. Click an entry to open it; use the filters to narrow down.

सर्व नोंदी तारखेनुसार. नोंद उघडण्यासाठी त्यावर क्लिक करा; फिल्टर वापरून शोधा.

Step by step
  1. Every entry in date order. Use the filters (date, type, head, amount) to narrow down.
  2. Click an entry to open it; edit, print or delete from there (or right-click a row).
  3. ✏ Bulk change: tick many entries and change their date, head or narration together. 🔁 Find & replace narration fixes a word in many narrations at once.
  4. 🖨 Print vouchers in bulk prints the ticked or filtered vouchers.
Buttons on this screen
🖨 Print vouchers in bulkPrint the filtered vouchers.
✏ Bulk changeChange date, head or narration of many entries at once.
🔁 Find & replace narrationChange a word in many narrations at once.

⚡ Bulk Receipts

Enter many members' receipts at once — the due amount fills in automatically.

अनेक सभासदांच्या पावत्या एकाच वेळी — थकबाकी आपोआप भरली जाते.

Step by step
  1. Choose Deposited into, the default date and mode.
  2. Every member is listed with the amount due. Type the amount received against each flat (leave others blank).
  3. Or: 📄 Download template → fill in Excel → 📥 Import filled template; or 📋 Paste from Excel.
  4. Photos: 📷 Collect cheques (front and back of each cheque) or 📷 Receipt book photo — AI fills the rows.
  5. Check the rows and press Save — one receipt per member is made.

📄 Download templateExcel template to fill many entries.
📥 Import filled templateImport the filled template.
📋 Paste from ExcelPaste receipts copied from Excel.
📷 Collect chequesPhotos of cheques → receipts awaiting clearing → deposit slip.
📷 Receipt book photoPhoto of a hand-written receipt book page or a collection list

⚡ Bulk Payments

Enter many payments at once.

अनेक पेमेंट एकाच वेळी नोंदवा.

Step by step
  1. Choose Paid from, default date and mode.
  2. Add one row per payment (head, amount, cheque no., narration) — or download the template, fill it and import it.
  3. Check and Save — one payment voucher per row.
Buttons on this screen
📄 Download templateExcel template to fill many entries.
📥 Import filled templateImport the filled template.

📝 Credit / Debit Notes

Step by step
  1. ➖ Credit note reduces what a member owes (e.g. excess charge, rebate). ➕ Debit note adds a charge outside the bill (e.g. repair done for one flat).
  2. Pick the member, head, amount and reason; save. It shows in the member ledger and dues.
Buttons on this screen
➖ Credit noteReduce what a member owes.
➕ Debit noteAdd a charge to a member outside the bill.

🧾 Vendor Bills

Step by step
  1. Record a supplier's bill when it is received (before paying): + Vendor bill → vendor, bill no., date, head, amount, GST / TDS.
  2. The vendor-wise table shows what is unpaid and how old it is.
  3. When you pay, choose the vendor in Payment — the bill is marked paid. 📥 Invoices from vendors shows bills sent by vendors from their link.

🔂 Recurring Payments

Step by step
  1. + Recurring payment for payments made every month (salary, security agency, AMC, lift).
  2. Each month choose Post for month and post them in one click; change any amount before posting.
  3. 💡 Suggestions finds payments that repeat in your books and offers to add them.

📆 Post-dated Cheques

Step by step
  1. + Post-dated cheque: member, cheque no., date, amount and bank.
  2. On the cheque date S4S reminds you; turn it into a receipt with one click when it is deposited.
Buttons on this screen
+ Post-dated chequeRecord a post-dated cheque received.

↩ Bounced Cheques

Step by step
  1. A cheque bounced? Open the receipt (Day Book or member ledger) and press ↩ Cheque bounced.
  2. S4S reverses the receipt and can add the bounce charges; the cheque is listed here with the date and reason.

AGM Checklist for Housing Societies

The annual general meeting has to be held by 30 September. Use this checklist to plan it from the close of the year to the final minutes.

Key dates for a year ending 31 March

ByWhat
15 MayAccounts finalised and handed to the statutory auditor (within 45 days)
31 JulyStatutory audit completed (within 4 months)
Mid-SeptemberAGM notice sent, with at least 14 clear days before the meeting
30 SeptemberAGM held (within 6 months of year end)

All of these dates are on our compliance calendar, with one-tap Google Calendar reminders.

Before the notice

  • Audited Income & Expenditure Account, Balance Sheet and audit report received.
  • Form O for the previous year's audit ready, if not already placed.
  • Budget for the next year prepared by the managing committee.
  • Names of auditors from the approved panel collected, with their fee quotes.
  • Managing committee meeting held to approve the agenda and the date, time and venue.

The notice

  • Send it at least 14 clear days before the meeting, or as your bye-laws require.
  • Include the date, time, venue and the full agenda. Attach or make available the audited accounts.
  • Mention the adjourned meeting time if quorum is not met.
  • Keep proof of delivery: signatures, email log or WhatsApp delivery, and a copy on the notice board.

Download our free AGM notice template.

A typical agenda

  1. Read and confirm the minutes of the previous general meeting.
  2. Receive and adopt the managing committee's annual report.
  3. Receive and adopt the audited accounts and the statutory audit report.
  4. Consider the audit rectification report (Form O) for the previous year.
  5. Appoint the statutory auditor for the current year and fix the remuneration.
  6. Approve the budget and any change in maintenance charges or interest rate.
  7. Any other item with the permission of the chair.

On the day

  • Attendance register signed by every member present.
  • Check quorum as per your bye-laws before starting.
  • Record each resolution with proposer, seconder and voting result.

After the meeting

  • Write the minutes and get them signed by the chairperson.
  • Implement the decisions: auditor appointment letter, revised bills, fund changes.
  • File the annual returns with the Registrar within the time allowed.

This guide is general information for managing committees, based on the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960, the rules made under it and the model bye-laws as of the date above. Your society's registered bye-laws and any later notification prevail. Call us before acting on a specific case.

FAQ

Common Questions

When must a housing society hold its AGM?

Within six months of the end of the financial year, that is by 30 September for a year ending 31 March.

How much notice is needed for a housing society AGM?

At least 14 clear days under the model bye-laws, unless your registered bye-laws say otherwise.

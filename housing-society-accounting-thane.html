Is a statutory audit compulsory for every housing society?

Yes. Every co-operative housing society in Maharashtra must get its accounts audited every year under the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act. The audit should be completed within four months of the end of the financial year, that is by 31 July, so the audited accounts can be placed before the AGM.

What is Form O and when is it due?

Form O is the audit rectification report. The managing committee must submit it within three months of the date of the audit report, explaining how each audit objection has been rectified, and place it before the general body.

Can you handle large complexes with multiple wings?

Yes. Many Thane complexes have several wings, shops and separate funds. We maintain wing-wise records where required and prepare combined financial statements for the society.

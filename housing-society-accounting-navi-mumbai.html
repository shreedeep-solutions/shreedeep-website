Society Accountants Based in Navi Mumbai

Running a co-operative housing society means maintenance bills every period, reconciled bank and cash accounts, an annual statutory audit, an AGM before 30 September, and income tax and TDS filings on time. For most managing committees, made up of volunteers with full-time jobs, that is a lot to keep track of.

Shreedeep Solutions handles the complete accounting and compliance cycle for housing societies across Navi Mumbai, working from our office in Sector-8, Airoli. Because we are local, committee members can reach us easily by phone or WhatsApp, and records move quickly between the society and our team.

Whether your society has 20 flats or several wings with shops, we prepare accounts that are audit-ready, explain them clearly to your committee, and keep track of every statutory deadline so nothing is missed. See all upcoming dates on our compliance calendar.

Can you take over our society's accounts from our current accountant?

Yes. We collect the records from your previous accountant, verify opening balances, bank balances and member dues, and then continue billing and accounts without a break, even in the middle of the year.

When is a housing society's income tax return due?

Co-operative housing societies file ITR-5. Because their accounts are audited under the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, the due date is 31 October. Filing on time is important to keep the deduction available to co-operative societies.

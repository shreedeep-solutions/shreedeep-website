Upcoming Due Dates

Housing society compliances under the MCS Act, TDS, and Income Tax Return deadlines for individuals and societies. Add any date to your Google Calendar in one tap.

CHS dates as per the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 and Model Bye-laws, for the financial year ending 31 March. The Form O date assumes the audit report is dated 31 July; it is actually due within 3 months of your society's audit report date. TDS and ITR dates are as per the Income-tax Act, 2025 (TDS returns in Form 138 for salary and Form 140 for non-salary). The Government sometimes extends deadlines, so please call us to confirm the latest position.
